# Formula

## Algebric Formula

$$
(a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2
$$

$$
(a+b)^2=a^2+b^2+2ab
$$

$$
(a-b)^2=a^2+b^2-2ab
$$

$$
(a+b+c)^2=a^2+b^2+c^2+2(ab+bc+ca)
$$

$$
a^3+b^3=(a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2)
$$

$$
a^3-b^3=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)
$$

$$
(a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc)=(a+b+c)(a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca)
$$
$$
\Rightarrow \text{When } a+b+c=0,\ \text{then } a^3+b^3+c^3=3abc
$$

## Binomial Equation
### $$(a+b)^n$$
$$(a+b)^n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}a^{\,n-k}b^{\,k}$$
$$
(a+b)^n=a^n+\binom{n}{1}a^{n-1}b+\binom{n}{2}a^{n-2}b^2+\cdots+\binom{n}{n-1}ab^{n-1}+b^n
$$
### $$(a-b)^n$$
$$
(a-b)^n = \sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}a^{n-k}(-b)^k
$$
$$(a-b)^n=a^n-\binom{n}{1}a^{n-1}b+\binom{n}{2}a^{n-2}b^2-\cdots+(-1)^n b^n$$
